# Naija Then & Now

**A public accountability dashboard tracking key Nigerian national indicators before and after May 29, 2023. No spin. Just the numbers.**

---

## What This Is

May 29, 2023 marked the beginning of a new federal administration in Nigeria. Naija Then & Now tracks what has changed — and what hasn't — across the indicators that matter most to everyday Nigerians.

This is not a political project. It is a data project. Every figure is sourced, every claim is cited, and the methodology is open for scrutiny.

---

## Indicators Tracked

- Inflation rate
- Fuel price
- Exchange rate (USD/NGN)
- Electricity supply (average daily hours)
- Unemployment rate
- Food price index
- External reserves
- Government debt
- Security incidents

Each indicator shows a **then** figure (as of May 29, 2023) and a **now** figure (latest available data), with source and date.

---

## Data Sources

All data is drawn from publicly available official and institutional sources including:

- National Bureau of Statistics (NBS)
- Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN)
- NNPC
- World Bank
- ACLED (security data)

Sources are cited inline on each data point.
